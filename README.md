# Salesforce B2B CRM Sales Pipeline

A hands-on Salesforce CRM project designed to manage B2B prospects, clients and sales opportunities through a structured sales pipeline.

## Project Overview

This project simulates a B2B software sales process using Salesforce. It covers the complete workflow from capturing a potential lead to managing the account, contact, opportunity and follow-up activities.

The project was built as a practical CRM implementation to understand how Salesforce can support sales teams with customer data management, opportunity tracking .

## CRM Workflow

```text
Lead
  ↓
Lead Qualification
  ↓
Account + Contact
  ↓
Opportunity
  ↓
Follow-up
  ↓
Sales Pipeline
  
```

## Salesforce Features Used

* Leads
* Accounts
* Contacts
* Opportunities
* Lead Conversion
* Custom Fields
* Tasks and Follow-ups
* Opportunity Stages

## Custom Fields

Created custom fields to support B2B sales tracking:

* Client Requirement
* Follow-up Date

These fields help sales teams understand customer requirements and track upcoming follow-up activities.

## Lead Management

Created and managed B2B leads containing:

* Contact information
* Company information
* Job title
* Lead source
* Client requirements
* Follow-up date

Qualified leads were converted into Salesforce Accounts, Contacts and Opportunities.

## Opportunity Management

Created opportunities to represent potential business deals and tracked them through different sales stages.

Example opportunities:

| Opportunity                   |    Amount | Stage          |
| ----------------------------- | --------: | -------------- |
| TechNova CRM Solution         | Some amount | Qualification  |
| CloudMatrix SaaS Subscription | some amount | Proposal       |


Opportunity records include expected deal value, close date, stage, lead source and next sales activity.

## Follow-up Management

Created follow-up activities for opportunities to track the next action required from the sales team.

Example:

**Follow-up:** Schedule requirements discussion with procurement team

This helps maintain a structured sales process and reduces missed follow-ups.


Salesforce reports and dashboards are used to analyze:

* Sales pipeline
* Opportunity value
* Opportunity stages
* Sales activity
* Lead information
